## Could not obtain / limitations

1. **Facebook login wall** — A login modal repeatedly appears on the page. Public cover, profile, Photos strip, and some posts remained viewable after dismissing it; deeper albums / “See all photos” often failed to load without authentication.
2. **No clean dedicated burger close-up** — Cover collage includes a burger near the phone screenshot chrome; crops were either letterboxed or mixed with other dishes. Burgers menu tab intentionally has **no mismatched dish photo**.
3. **No separate high-res daytime storefront** from Facebook alone — Night exterior was available; daytime storefront appears only in third-party listing collage (kept as archive, not primary gallery).
4. **CDN tokens expire** — Facebook `scontent` URLs include short-lived `oh`/`oe` tokens. Local WebP copies are the durable website assets.
5. **Unrelated posts skipped** — Music-quote / inspirational graphics that appeared in the feed (`post-food-d`, `post-food-e`) were **not** used.
6. **Prior hotlinks** — Old `findglocal.com` mirrors returned HTTP 403 and were removed from the app.
